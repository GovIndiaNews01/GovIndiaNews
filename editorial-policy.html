1. Mission & Editorial Independence

GovIndiaNews operates as an independent news platform dedicated to simplifying public policy, government job notifications, and global economic developments. Our mission is to provide clear, accessible, and verified information to citizens, jobseekers, and policy observers.

Our editorial decisions are made independently by our editorial desk without influence from advertisers, commercial sponsors, political organizations, or external PR agencies. No advertiser or commercial partner has veto power over our reporting.

2. Primary Source Sourcing Standards

To ensure total reliability, GovIndiaNews enforces a strict primary-source rule across all coverage areas:

  • Government Schemes: Sourced directly from official Ministry Gazettes, Press Information Bureau (PIB) releases, and government department portals (.gov.in / .nic.in).
  • Government Jobs: Verified against official employment news publications, public service commission notifications (e.g., UPSC, SSC, State PSCs), and official recruitment boards.
  • Global Finance: Grounded in official publications from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Ministry of Finance, Federal Reserve, World Bank, and IMF.

3. Fact Verification Workflow

Before any article is cleared for publication on GovIndiaNews, it undergoes a structured three-tier review process:

  1. Documentary Cross-Checking: All dates, eligibility numbers, salary bands, and subsidy percentages are cross-checked against official PDF notifications.
  2. Contextual Clarity Audit: Complex bureaucratic language is translated into plain, readable language without altering legal meaning.
  3. Link Verification: Direct hyperlinks to official government application portals are verified for security and authenticity.

4. Clear Separation of Editorial & Commercial Operations

GovIndiaNews maintains a strict wall between editorial content and programmatic advertising:

  • Advertisements displayed on our site are clearly labeled as sponsored or ad units.
  • We do not accept paid "sponsored articles" that masquerade as independent journalism.
  • Ad networks do not dictate editorial selection, article tone, or publication schedules.

5. AI Assistance Policy

Where AI tools are used for grammar formatting or language editing, all outputs undergo human editorial review, factual verification, and final clearance before publication. No article is generated or published without human editorial oversight.

6. Non-Partisan Political Neutrality

GovIndiaNews maintains non-partisan editorial reporting. We report on policies, welfare initiatives, and economic data objectively, avoiding political bias, speculation, or partisan commentary.

7. Attribution & Plagiarism Standards

Plagiarism is strictly prohibited. When referencing external research, statistical surveys, or institutional reports, GovIndiaNews provides clear attribution and direct source citations.

8. Editorial Accountability & Reader Feedback

We welcome reader feedback, error reports, and constructive critiques. If a factual error occurs, we correct it transparently in accordance with our Corrections Policy.
