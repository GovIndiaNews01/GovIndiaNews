1. Commitment to Factual Precision

Accuracy is the foundational cornerstone of GovIndiaNews. Because our coverage directly impacts citizens seeking welfare scheme benefits, job applicants reviewing recruitment rules, and readers assessing economic trends, we enforce rigorous fact-checking protocols before publishing any article.

2. Core Verification Methodology

Every factual assertion, statistic, deadline, and eligibility criteria published on GovIndiaNews must be substantiated by primary documentation. Our researchers and editors adhere to a strict verification checklist:

  • Cross-Referencing: Claims made in press releases must be cross-referenced against official gazette PDFs or ministry notifications.
  • Data Point Consistency: Numbers such as vacancy counts, age upper limits, pay band scale numbers, and financial outlay figures are verified digit-by-digit.
  • Temporal Validity: Dates are checked to ensure reports differentiate between past policies, current active windows, and proposed future amendments.

3. Primary Document Hierarchy

GovIndiaNews establishes a clear hierarchy of acceptable source materials:

  1. Level 1 (Primary Gazette & Official Portals): Official Gazette of India, State Government Extraordinaries, UPSC/SSC Official Notifications, RBI Bulletins.
  2. Level 2 (Institutional Releases): Press Information Bureau (PIB) briefings, World Bank/IMF Official Reports, Central Bank Press Communiqués.
  3. Level 3 (Secondary Sources): Secondary news reports are used strictly for lead identification and are NEVER published until independently verified against Level 1 or Level 2 documents.

4. Verification of Government Recruitment & Schemes

Public sector recruitment announcements and scheme guides are especially prone to online misinformation and fake circulars. GovIndiaNews verifies:

  • Official notification advertisement numbers (e.g., Advt No. XX/2026)
  • Authentic domain URLs (.gov.in / .nic.in / .ac.in) for online application portals
  • Exact opening and closing dates for online registration and fee payment

5. Verification of Global Financial & Economic Reports

Financial articles detailing macroeconomic policy, interest rate benchmark adjustments, and inflation data are verified against official releases from issuing central banks (e.g., Reserve Bank of India, US Federal Reserve, European Central Bank) or international multilateral institutions.

6. Handling Unverified Rumors & Misinformation

GovIndiaNews does not publish unverified social media leaks, unofficial exam date predictions, or unconfirmed policy rumors. If viral misinformation circulates regarding a major recruitment or scheme, our editorial team publishes factual clarification pieces pointing directly to official government denials or PIB Fact Check releases.

7. Transparent Correction & Update Protocol

If an error or outdated figure is discovered after publication, our editorial desk acts immediately to correct the record transparently in accordance with our Corrections Policy.

Report a Factual Discrepancy:
If you spot an inaccuracy in any of our reports, please notify our fact-checking team at factcheck@govindianews.com or submit a note via contact.html.
