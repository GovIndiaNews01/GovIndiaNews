1. Non-Governmental Entity Notice

GovIndiaNews is a privately operated news and information desk. We are NOT a government department, ministry, agency, public sector enterprise, or official commission of the Government of India or any state or union territory government. We do not operate under the mandate or authorization of any government authority.

2. General Informational Nature of Content

All content published on GovIndiaNews—including summaries, breakdowns, analysis pieces, tables, and guides regarding Government Schemes, Government Jobs, and Global Finance—is provided solely for general educational and journalistic purposes.

While our editorial team rigorously verifies all reports against primary public notifications and press releases, government policies, recruitment criteria, and market numbers are subject to official revision without notice. Content on GovIndiaNews must not be construed as official gazette notifications.

3. No Application Processing or Fee Collection

GovIndiaNews does NOT process job applications, admit card downloads, scheme enrollments, or financial subsidy transfers. We do NOT collect application fees, processing charges, registration deposits, or payment details under any pretext.

Any portal or individual claiming to represent GovIndiaNews while requesting payments or credentials is fraudulent and should be reported immediately to our grievance officer and law enforcement authorities.

4. Verification Obligation of Readers

Readers are strictly advised to cross-verify all scheme benefits, eligibility criteria, age limits, qualification requirements, examination dates, and submission deadlines on official government domain portals (.gov.in or .nic.in) prior to taking administrative or financial actions.

5. Financial & Economic Reporting Disclaimer

Reports in our Global Finance category detail central bank interest rate policies, inflation indicators, foreign exchange trends, and world economic forecasts for informational background. Nothing published on GovIndiaNews constitutes financial planning advice, stock brokerage recommendations, or investment advice.

6. Recruitment & Employment Disclaimer

GovIndiaNews is an independent news reporting platform and does not guarantee job placements, interview calls, or recruitment selections. All public sector recruitment processes are strictly conducted by official commissions (e.g., UPSC, SSC, State PSCs, RRBs) through their official web portals.

7. No Legal, Tax, or Professional Advice

No content on this portal shall be interpreted as legal, tax, or professional advisory services. Users requiring specialized legal or financial guidance must consult certified professionals registered with appropriate statutory bodies.

8. External Links & Third-Party Websites

Articles on GovIndiaNews frequently link to official government websites, institutional portals, and primary source documents. GovIndiaNews does not control or endorse external websites and assumes no liability for their content, availability, or operational policies.

9. Limitation of Liability

GovIndiaNews, its owners, editors, and operational staff expressly disclaim all liability for direct, indirect, incidental, or consequential losses or damages resulting from reliance on information published on this website.

10. Reporting Inaccuracies

We welcome reader feedback to maintain pristine factual accuracy. If you identify a discrepancy or outdated detail in any report, please submit an error report via our Corrections Policy page or email corrections@govindianews.com.

Mandatory Reader Notice:
Always confirm official recruitment and scheme notifications on official department websites (.gov.in or .nic.in) before submitting applications or making financial decisions.
