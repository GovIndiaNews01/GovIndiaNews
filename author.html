About Akash Singh Solanki

Akash Singh Solanki is an independent digital publisher, policy research editor, and the founder of GovIndiaNews. Driven by a commitment to public transparency, Akash established GovIndiaNews to break down complex government welfare scheme notifications, public sector recruitment guidelines, and economic policies into clear, accurate, and accessible guides for citizens across India.

• Primary Areas of Focus: Government Welfare Schemes (PM Awas Yojana 2.0, PMAYMIS, Awaassoft), Public Sector & Defense Recruitment Standards (Indian Army 1600m PFT, Combined Physical Test - CPT 2026), CPGRAMS Online Grievance Redressal, and Public Administration Verification.

Primary Verification & Zero Fake Claims Policy

Under the editorial direction of Akash Singh Solanki, GovIndiaNews operates with an unwavering commitment to 100% factual accuracy and complete transparency:

  • Zero Fake Claims Policy: GovIndiaNews makes no exaggerated claims, fake job promises, or guaranteed approval declarations. All timelines, subsidy calculations, and physical fitness criteria are cited directly from official government documentation.
  • Primary Source Cross-Checking: Every guide published on GovIndiaNews is verified against official Gazettes of India, Press Information Bureau (PIB) releases, and official portal domains (ending in .gov.in or .nic.in).
  • Strict Non-Governmental Identity: Akash Singh Solanki and GovIndiaNews explicitly disclose that GovIndiaNews is a privately operated news portal. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorized by any government department or ministry.
