1. Introduction & Scope

GovIndiaNews ("we," "us," or "our") operates the independent online publication located at https://www.govindianews.com/. We are committed to protecting the privacy, security, and personal data of our visitors, readers, and user community. This Privacy Policy governs all pages, features, and services accessible through our domain.

This policy details how GovIndiaNews collects, processes, stores, and protects data when you access our editorial analysis of Government Schemes, Government Jobs, and Global Finance. By visiting or using GovIndiaNews, you acknowledge the data practices described in this document.

2. Information We Collect

GovIndiaNews is designed as a privacy-focused news platform that minimizes personal data collection. We categorize collected information into Voluntary Information and Technical Information:

  • Voluntary Contact Information: When you submit inquiries through our contact forms or communicate with our editorial desk via email, we collect your name, email address, message subject, and inquiry details.
  • Grievance & Feedback Submissions: If you file a formal editorial feedback or grievance notice, we collect contact credentials and supporting documentation required to process your submission under relevant Indian digital media regulations.

3. Technical Data & Web Analytics

When you navigate GovIndiaNews, our web servers automatically log standard Internet protocol data sent by your web browser. This includes:

  • IP (Internet Protocol) address in an anonymized format
  • Browser type, version, and language settings
  • Operating system and device architecture
  • Referring webpage URLs and exit pages
  • Timestamped page requests and reading duration

We analyze aggregate technical logs exclusively to optimize website performance, improve reading speed, monitor server capacity, and prevent illegal network intrusion.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

GovIndiaNews utilizes browser Cookies and HTML5 Local Storage to enhance reading performance and preserve site preferences. These mechanisms do not access your local file system or private storage:

  • Essential Preference Storage: We use HTML5 Local Storage to remember your dark mode preference (govindianews_theme_pref) across sessions.
  • Performance Cookies: Aggregate session cookies ensure smooth navigation across category pages without requiring repeated asset downloads.

5. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To keep our independent reporting freely accessible to the public, GovIndiaNews may display non-intrusive programmatic advertisements via Google AdSense or authorized ad networks:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on user visits to GovIndiaNews and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.

6. How We Use Collected Data

GovIndiaNews strictly limits data utilization to the following legal and operational purposes:

  • To render, maintain, and optimize website layout and technical performance
  • To respond promptly to editorial inquiries, error reports, and user feedback
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and protect against malicious cyber activities
  • To satisfy legal requirements under applicable Indian legislation, including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act

7. Data Sharing & Legal Disclosures

GovIndiaNews does not sell, rent, trade, or lease user personal data to third-party brokers, marketers, or data aggregators. We only disclose personal information under these strict conditions:

  • Legal Mandate: When compelled by valid court orders, government law enforcement subpoenas, or legal statutory requirements under Indian law.
  • Infrastructure Providers: With trusted technical cloud service providers operating under strict confidentiality contracts solely to host and serve our static infrastructure.

8. Data Retention & Security Measures

We apply robust administrative, technical, and physical security controls to safeguard all information transmitted to our platform. All data transmissions are encrypted using standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.

Contact messages and editorial inquiries are retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the request or satisfy statutory recordkeeping obligations, after which they are securely purged.

9. User Privacy Rights (DPDP Act Compliance)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and applicable international standards, users possess specific data privacy rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request confirmation regarding whether we hold personal contact details associated with your email address.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You may request the correction of inaccurate personal data or the deletion of your contact records from our inquiry database.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where data processing relies on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting past processing validity.

10. Children's Privacy

GovIndiaNews is a general audience news portal providing policy and economic analysis. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal data from children under the age of 18. If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has provided contact details to us, please notify us immediately at privacy@govindianews.com for prompt erasure.

11. External Links Disclaimer

Our articles frequently reference official government portals, public service commissions, and international finance institutions. GovIndiaNews has no control over, and assumes no responsibility for, the privacy practices or content of third-party external websites.

12. Changes to Privacy Policy

GovIndiaNews reserves the right to revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological changes, statutory updates, or evolving editorial operations. All updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" timestamp.
